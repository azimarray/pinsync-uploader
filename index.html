Privacy Policy
Internal Tool

Content scheduling and publishing, built for The Science Sunnah.

This is an internal tool created and owned by The Science Sunnah to manage, schedule, and publish educational video content across our own social accounts. It is not a public product and is not available for sign-up.

What it does

Takes a finished video from our internal library and publishes it to our own connected social accounts using each platform's official API.

Who uses it

Only The Science Sunnah's own team, acting on our own authorized accounts. No external users or third-party data are involved.

How it connects

Uses each platform's official OAuth login flow to request access to our own accounts only — nothing is scraped or accessed without authorization.
