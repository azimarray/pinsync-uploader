Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

The Science Sunnah Video Uploader ("the App") is an internal tool built and operated by The Science Sunnah to schedule and publish our own educational video content to our own social media accounts. This policy explains how the App handles data.

What the App accesses

The App connects only to social media accounts that are owned and explicitly authorized by The Science Sunnah, including our Pinterest Business account, using each platform's official OAuth authorization flow. Access is limited to the scopes required to upload and publish video content (for example, creating Pins on Pinterest). The App does not access, request, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user or account.

What data we collect

  • We do not collect, process, or store any data from third-party users.
  • The App has no public sign-up, login, or user-facing data entry — it is used exclusively by The Science Sunnah's own team.
  • The only data involved is our own video content and the OAuth access/refresh tokens needed to publish that content to our own accounts.

How we store data

API access tokens and credentials are stored securely on a local, private machine controlled by The Science Sunnah and are never transmitted to, or stored on, any third-party server. Video files are stored locally prior to upload and are not hosted on any public or shared platform by this App.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data with third parties. The App communicates exclusively with the official APIs of the platforms we publish to, for the sole purpose of publishing our own content to our own accounts.

Data retention and deletion

Access tokens are retained only as long as needed to operate the App and can be revoked at any time by The Science Sunnah through the relevant platform's developer settings (for example, Pinterest's app permissions page) or by deleting the locally stored credential files.
